Mae, just hear me out —

honestly, Mae — there are nights when the distance feels so, so heavy. quiet, alone, and all i want is to just sit next to you, say absolutely nothing. just be in the same space as you. that's it. but somehow, that feels like everything.

people always ask, "how do you do the whole long distance thing?" and honestly, i never really know how to explain it properly. what i do know is — loving you from this far away still feels like the easiest thing i've ever done. because you always make everything feel worth it. always, Mae.

there are small things i hold onto on the hard days: the way you laugh and it somehow still fills the whole room, even through a screen. the voice notes i replay over and over — way more than i'd ever admit. and imagining the day i won't have to say "good night" and then disconnect — that thought alone genuinely gets me through everything.

so here's what i need you to know, Mae. the distance is temporary. the waiting is temporary. but what i feel for you — big, stubborn, and refusing to go anywhere — it's not going anywhere. not because of distance. not because of time. not because of anything.

one day i'll actually be there. and i'll hold you like i'm making up for every second i couldn't. until then — i'm still here, Mae. yours. from right here.
